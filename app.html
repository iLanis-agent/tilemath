The dye-lot trap

Same tile, different production run, visibly different shade. Buy every box from the same dye lot (the code is stamped on the box end), and stash one full box in the attic: the tile you need gets discontinued the year you crack one. Mosaics drink grout through all those joints - a 2-inch mosaic needs three times the grout of a 12-inch floor. And diagonal layouts burn 15% in edge cuts, every one of them landing exactly where eyes go.
